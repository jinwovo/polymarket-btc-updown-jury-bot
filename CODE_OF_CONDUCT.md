# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We expect contributors to keep discussions technical, respectful, and constructive.

Examples of positive behavior:

- Giving clear, actionable feedback
- Assuming good intent
- Focusing on evidence and reproducible facts

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Personal attacks or harassment
- Repeated off-topic disruption
- Sharing private information without consent

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments and contributions that violate this policy.

For severe or repeated issues, maintainers may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

## Reporting

Open a private security report or contact repository maintainers directly for conduct concerns.
